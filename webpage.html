Bachelor Thesis

Title: Simulation of Hermite processes

Abstract: Hermite processes are self-similar stochastic processes with stationary increments and long-range dependence. The simplest example is fractional Brownian motion. The aim of the thesis is to study several methods for simulating Hermite processes, such as approximations based on Wiener–Itô integrals and wavelet expansions, to implement them, and to compare their accuracy and computational cost.
